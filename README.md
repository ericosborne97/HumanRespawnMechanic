# HumanRespawnMechanic

> **Does everyone respawn eventually?** Consider my simulation and theory. But as always it's just a theory.

---

## The Theory

Consider the following: a person goes into anesthesia for a surgery. The surgery takes three hours. To the person, however, no time has passed—his consciousness is shut off. Now consider that at some point the universe will reproduce the exact makeup of the part that makes you conscious. Put all the parts that make you what you are in a bag and shake it enough times and it will eventually make you. The odds of this happening is **1/infinity**.

Now consider that when you are dead, you will have no perception of time. So it doesn't matter whether it takes an almost infinite time to respawn. To you, that will be instant.

---

## Supporting Facts

- **Anesthesia** acts as a localized time-dilation effect on consciousness
- The universe is finite but effectively infinite in its combinatorial possibilities
- Death removes the subjective experience of duration entirely

---

## Simulation Parameters

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Consciousness parts | ~10²⁸ atoms |
| Shaking iterations | ∞ (or 2^∞) |
| Perceived respawn time | Instant |

> **TL;DR:** If the universe is big enough and you're dead long enough, you'll eventually wake up again. And since death feels like a single moment to you, it might as well be now.
